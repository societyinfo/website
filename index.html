The idea

A space to connect knowledge, experience and people.

Nepalese people in Luxembourg bring diverse professional, academic, entrepreneurial and life experiences. This initiative explores how we can create a voluntary platform where those experiences can be shared for the benefit of individuals and the wider community.

01

03

Voluntary

First practical initiative

Language & Culture Exchange

A simple way to start: create opportunities for people who know Nepali to share it, while people who grew up in Luxembourg can share French or Luxembourgish.

नेपाली↔Français
नेपाली↔Lëtzebuergesch

Rather than starting as a formal language school, the first pilot could be small, friendly conversation groups combined with cultural exchange.

Possible pilot

90 minutes · 4–8 people

  • 15 min Welcome & introductions
  • 20 min Language pairing
  • 30 min Small-group conversation
  • 15 min Cultural exchange
  • 10 min Feedback & next steps

First step

Let’s discover the expertise already around us.

Before launching many activities, we would like to build a voluntary community and expertise roster. This will help us understand who is interested, what people know, and where people would like to contribute.
